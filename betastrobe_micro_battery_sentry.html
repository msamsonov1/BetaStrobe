Sub-Atomic Betavoltaic Degradation Physics

Non-Ionizing Energy Loss (NIEL), Shockley-Read-Hall Trap Recombination & Admittance Degradation in Silicon Carbide

01

Messenger-Kirkwood Frenkel Defect Generation

Continuous β decay from Tritium ($E_{\text{avg}} = 5.7\,\text{keV}, E_{\max} = 18.6\,\text{keV}$) or Nickel-63 ($E_{\text{avg}} = 17.4\,\text{keV}, E_{\max} = 66.9\,\text{keV}$) impacts 4H-SiC. Electrons exceeding the threshold displacement energy ($T_d \approx 20\,\text{eV}$ for Carbon, $35\,\text{eV}$ for Silicon) knock atoms into interstitial sites, creating primary knock-on atoms (PKAs) and Frenkel pairs:

$$N_{\text{def}}(t) = \int_{0}^{t} \left[ \int_{E_d}^{E_{\max}} \Phi_\beta(E, \tau) \cdot \sigma_{\text{disp}}(E) \cdot \nu_{\text{Kinchin}}(E) \, dE \right] d\tau$$

Where $\sigma_{\text{disp}}(E)$ is the relativistic McKinley-Feshbach scattering cross-section and $\nu_{\text{Kinchin}}$ is the modified Kinchin-Pease damage multiplier.

02

Shockley-Read-Hall (SRH) Defect Clustering & Conductance

Accumulated Carbon vacancies ($V_C$) migrate into stable divacancies and $Z_{1/2}$ centers located $0.65\,\text{eV}$ below the conduction band. These deep traps pin the Fermi level and decimate the minority carrier diffusion length ($L_n, L_p$), causing the small-signal AC conductance $G(\omega)$ across the depletion layer to collapse:

$$G(\omega) = \frac{q \cdot A \cdot \mu_n \cdot n_0}{W_{\text{dep}}} + \sum_{k} \frac{q \cdot N_{t,k} \cdot \omega^2 \tau_k}{1 + \omega^2 \tau_k^2} \cdot \left[1 - \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta E_k}{k_B T}\right)\right]$$

As deep defect clusters coalesce, $\tau_k$ diverges and $W_{\text{dep}}$ shrinks, manifesting as an identifiable, steep gradient deviation $\frac{dG}{dQ} \ll \left.\frac{dG}{dQ}\right|_{\text{nominal}}$.

03

Phase-Space Inversion & Dynamic Aging Metric

Unlike simplistic coulomb counting, plotting accumulated harvest charge $Q(t) = \int I_{\text{harvest}} dt$ against small-signal AC conductance $G(t)$ provides an orthogonal state observer invariant to external load resistance fluctuations:

$$\Psi_{\text{sentry}}(Q, G) = \nabla_{(Q, G)} \left| G_{\text{measured}} - \left( G_0 - \beta_{\text{vol}} \cdot Q^{\alpha} \right) \right| > \Gamma_{\text{hazard}}$$

When $\Psi_{\text{sentry}}$ exceeds the threshold $\Gamma_{\text{hazard}}$, the firmware detects that internal lattice destruction has surpassed isotope radio-decay, predicting imminent cell thermal-electric runaway.
